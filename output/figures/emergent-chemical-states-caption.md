Removing polar mechanical action while retaining activity-dependent tension and adhesion restores formation. This supports necessity within the tested full-coupling combination and sufficiency of polar mechanics alone, limited to this starting geometry, parameters, and horizon. **D.** Three independently developed histories are continued from time 90 with full coupling either enabled or kept off. Every arm retains contrast over time 135–150. Points are individual histories, not means or confidence intervals; connecting lines identify paired interventions.

The strongest supported conclusion is that chemical differences can emerge without prescribed fate labels, while polarity-dependent mechanics restricts their initiation in the tested regime without erasing developed patterns. These results do not establish autonomous or inherited biological cell identity, or that polarity mechanics is required for differentiation to emerge.
