## What was tested

The study uses three existing mature developmental histories (7–9), with two selected recipients nested in each: cells 22/23, 19/20 and 29/30. It adds **zero developmental histories**. The original sham physical context and sham-based recipient matching supply this first moving confirmation; it does not repeat the entire frozen factorial.

At t=510, the paired chemical allocations have exactly the same recipient activator/inhibitor concentrations, complete physical source, volumes and conservative transport operator. Each species' surrounding and whole-network amounts match initially. Species allocation among other cells differs. Chemistry, mechanics, polarity, dilution and contact transport then co-evolve for 60 simulation-time units. The pulse is applied at t=570 from each actual new fine endpoint, with no second matching intervention. Each background has its own continuing unpulsed control.

The 96 new paths comprise 24 waiting paths and 72 delayed-response paths at dt=0.00375 and 0.001875. There are 48 pulse paths across numerical levels, representing 24 fine pulse labels. The pulses remove either 10% of recipient activator or a common activator amount equal to 10% of the smaller actual recipient amount across the paired backgrounds. Both use actual cell volumes. Inhibitor and other cells remain unchanged by the pulse. Repeated doses, recipients and timesteps are not developmental replicates.

## Interpretation and next test

The supported conclusion is **selective context-sensitive chemical behavior in mature tissue despite ongoing mechanical reorganization**. Initially equal recipient chemistry and species budgets do not prevent later differences. Agreement with the corresponding frozen classifications shows that the tested response differences do not require geometry to remain frozen. It does not prove that mechanics causes those differences: surrounding chemistry, local state, transport and derived material properties evolve together.

Recipient chemistry and species amounts differ again by the challenge time. Thus this assay does not establish a response difference at equal contemporaneous local state or a continuously matched budget. It also does not establish nearest-neighbor specificity, autonomous/inherited cell identities, new carry-corrected development or a many-cell continuum limit. The three histories retain their original developmental provenance; continuing them does not add new replication.

The next focused control is to **match recipient activator/inhibitor again at the challenge time**, using paired own-background controls and the existing timestep gates. A shared endpoint physical context would additionally separate the surrounding chemical-allocation effect from differing current geometry. Immediate reset transients must remain distinguished from a later settled challenge. These controls are proposed, not launched here.
