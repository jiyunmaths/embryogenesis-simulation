# Moving challenge-time amount matching: independent completed review

The completed experiment is **accepted at its tested scope**. Immediate chemical responses remain sensitive to the surrounding allocation when chemistry, cell shapes, contacts, volumes, polarity and mechanics evolve together. The paired preparations start with identical recipient activator/inhibitor, surrounding and whole-network amounts of each species, and physical fields. Above-screen response differences occur in **1/2, 2/2 and 2/2 recipients** in histories 7, 8 and 9, respectively. Both timesteps and the corresponding accepted frozen comparisons give the same six classifications.

This confirms that the tested immediate response differences do not depend on keeping geometry frozen. Unequal starting local chemistry or starting species totals are not necessary for these effects. It supports network-supported context sensitivity in mature tissue; it does not demonstrate autonomous or inherited biological identities, or organization from fresh zygotes.

## What was compared

All six previously selected recipients were retained, including the below-screen cell 23: cells 22/23 in history 7, 19/20 in history 8, and 29/30 in history 9. The primary references were M=S for local chemistry, B=S for surrounding species amounts, and P=S for physical context. S/R designate the two surrounding chemical allocation backgrounds. This is one prespecified slice of the [accepted frozen factorial](pulse_time_amount_response_assessment.md), not a moving repeat of its reciprocal references.

Each path starts at t=570 from the actual reviewed fine, unpulsed waiting endpoint. Physical fields, target volumes, polarity, lineage, random state, phase carry and rounding state are inherited. Exact accepted rounded-state chemistry is installed as an explicit preparation intervention. Its chemical correction is zero only at this reset and is retained thereafter. The matched contexts have equal recipient concentrations and capacities, so their −10% activator pulses also remove equal amounts. Inhibitor is unchanged by the pulse.

There is **no settling interval**: the reset and pulse are immediate, followed by free co-evolution to t=630. Every pulse is compared with its own continuing moving control. Amounts are matched only initially; reactions, evolving conservative transport and volume dilution subsequently act freely. Common physical fields and constitutive parameters do not require equal activity-derived tension or adhesion after the chemistry changes.

The design comprises 12 chemical contexts × pulse/control × two timesteps (0.00375/0.001875): **48 new moving paths**, 24 labeled starts and 24 distinct initial-value problems, each repeated at two timesteps. The reporting units remain **three existing histories, zero new histories**. Cells, backgrounds, interventions and numerical repeats are nested observations.

## Completed response results

For each allocation, the recipient's two-species response is the signed log concentration ratio between pulse and its own control, divided by the actual log pulse amplitude, `abs(log(0.9))`. E is the time-and-species RMS of the difference between the R and S responses over all 60 units. The predeclared screen is **E>0.01**. It is an operational response screen, not a biological identity threshold or a statistical significance test.

The frozen comparison uses exactly M=S/B=S/P=S, with identical chemical interventions and initial capacities/operators.

These changes, and the table's ratios of E, are descriptive. They are not additive causal fractions attributable to mechanics, and agreement of classifications is not a formal equivalence test.

All **12 fine pulse paths** recover toward their own controls under the sustained predeclared threshold. Recipient and network recovery times span **2.70–5.40 model-time units**, with at least 24 subsequent units checked. Both timestep levels recover, and every paired recovery-time discrepancy is zero. Maximum final network log RMS from control is **5.03e-5**, and maximum late-window RMS is **5.26e-5**. Operational recovery is therefore not exact return; small residual effects remain. E integrates the response window and must not be interpreted as a response difference maintained at the final time.

All 48 paths retain chemical contrast at every saved observation: the smallest whole-window log-activator SD is 1.359, against 0.1. This is maintenance of inherited and intervened mature states, not evidence of fresh initiation.

## Evolving amounts and physical context

Initial paired species-amount closure is at most **2.22e-16**, below 2e-13, including immediately after the matched pulse. Later amounts are not clamped. In the fine unpulsed controls, final R/S whole-network species-amount ratios range from **0.9206 to 1.0886**; maximum relative separation during the window is **12.56%**. The test excludes unequal initial amounts as a necessary explanation; it does not exclude a contribution from later amount differences.

Geometry and transport evolve. Across the twelve fine controls, total pair conductance declines **4.22–7.20%**, while the aggregate's endpoint axis-ratio change has magnitude at most **0.187%**. These are measured changes in mature tissue, with modest aggregate shape evolution. They do not establish robustness during substantial remodeling or cleavage. Chemical allocation can change activity-derived mechanics, and these observations do not isolate transport from tension, adhesion or polarity as the cause of a response effect.

## Interpretation and next priority

The supported result is **immediate allocation-sensitive chemical behavior in mature tissue despite live mechanics, starting from equal local chemical state and species totals**. The response depends on the surrounding coupled system under these preparations. It does not yet establish nearest-neighbor specificity, settled behavior after rematching, autonomous/inherited identity, fresh zygote-to-identity development or a continuum limit. All three histories retain their original developmental provenance.

The next major priority is [fresh-zygote development with the current numerical method](plan.md#next-scientific-priority-and-decision-rules). The current mature GPU backend excludes active cleavage. First qualify an isolated development backend for progressive cytokinesis, changing cell count, conservative daughter chemical amounts, numerical correction/carry inheritance and restart during division; then qualify a repeated-cleavage pilot before launching new independent histories with matched feedback/polarity controls. The completed coordinator has exited and no GPU compute jobs were reported at review. No new scientific job is launched by this assessment.

Original protocol, result files, thresholds, pending-review flags, shared sources and accepted parents remain unchanged. This separate assessment records independent acceptance at the tested scope; it does not promote the broader historical results ledger or revise manuscript numerical tables.
