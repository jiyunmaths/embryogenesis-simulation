# Challenge-time amount matching: independent completed review

The completed experiment is **accepted at its tested scope**. Spatial chemical allocation changes immediate recipient pulse responses even when starting recipient activator/inhibitor, surrounding species totals, whole-network species totals and physical context are equal. Above-screen effects occur in **1/2, 2/2 and 2/2 recipients** in histories 7, 8 and 9, respectively, under both local-state references, both amount references and both shared physical contexts. All pulses recover toward their own continuing controls.

The newly detected case is cell 22 in history 7. Matching total amounts increases its response effect above the screen, whereas equal local chemistry alone left it below. Unequal starting chemical totals are therefore not necessary for the detected allocation-sensitive responses. This supports chemical behavior shaped by the surrounding network during a reset; it does not establish autonomous or inherited biological cell identities.

Each response contains activator and inhibitor log ratios to its own continuing control, divided by the actual log-pulse magnitude; the RMS averages over the two species. The inherited strict **E > 0.01** criterion is an operational screen, not statistical significance or a percentage concentration change. Below-screen does not establish equivalence.

**Cell 22 becomes above-screen after amount matching.** Its smallest amount-matched E, 0.018698, is well separated from 0.01 and the maximum numerical E discrepancy of 2.24e-7. Both measured amount levels give this classification. Cell 23 increases in response magnitude but remains below screen. Thus matching amounts does not merely reduce every previously observed difference.

Cells 19/29 retain detected effects, although amount matching lowers their E relative to corresponding unmatched-budget preparations. Cells 20/30 also retain detected effects, with larger responses under local reference S than R. Their two local-reference magnitudes differ substantially, even though classification agrees. These are responses during immediate chemical preparation relaxation; they do not replace the earlier accepted below-screen native delayed-response results for cells 20/30.

Matched-minus-unmatched budget effects are reconstructed as differences of signed contrast waveforms **before** taking RMS. Their largest descriptive magnitude is 0.017435. This is not subtraction of scalar E values and does not yield an additive causal percentage attributable to dosage. Uniform surrounding scaling preserves each species' allocation fractions but changes local activator/inhibitor ratios and reaction kinetics. The intervention consequently tests conditional allocation sensitivity rather than a unique mechanistic decomposition. Physical-context and interaction effects remain descriptive, with maximum RMS 0.004521 and 0.003820.

All **168 labeled fine pulses**, representing 96 distinct physical pulse problems, recover toward their own continuing controls. Target and whole-network recovery times range from 0.45 to 5.40 units. Recovery requires every remaining saved observation to stay within its specified threshold with at least 24 units of follow-up. Timesteps and reference methods agree exactly on the 0.15-unit observation grid. Maximum late whole-network log RMS from the control is 4.31e-11. This finite-window recovery is not proof of equilibrium or a pulse-induced lasting identity.

The preparation changes immediate behavior without creating new final contextual states in this window. For each chemical allocation and shared physical context, native, locally reset and both amount-matched reference controls return to the same saved final network state within 1.78e-15 in maximum log concentration. Paired allocations remain different: cells 20/30 recover the approximately 17.5–20.6-fold activator separations seen in the parent endpoint control. All controls retain strong between-cell contrast; the minimum whole-window log-activator SD is 1.3521. This is mature-state maintenance and recovery, not new formation from a zygote.

Initial surrounding/global amount agreement is verified to a maximum relative error of 4.44e-16. Subsequent divergence is an outcome of free reactions and transport. By the end, matched-arm global R/S activator amount ratios range from 0.9200 to 1.0895, and inhibitor ratios from 0.9284 to 1.0889. Consequently, the 60-unit response contrast must not be described as operating at equal totals throughout. Equal initial totals are sufficient for the tested response differences to emerge, but this assay does not eliminate later amount differences as contributors.

## Interpretation and next priority

The accepted conclusion is **spatial chemical allocation affects immediate response despite equal initial recipient chemistry, species totals and transport geometry**. Unequal starting totals cannot be a necessary explanation for the detected effects in cells 19/20/29/30, and amount matching reveals an additional effect in cell 22. This provides stronger support for behavior sustained by collective chemical context than local concentration labels alone.

The conclusion remains conditional on an immediate chemical reset and a frozen physical context. Equalities hold at preparation time; concentrations and amounts then diverge. The restored contextual endpoints and complete pulse recoveries support reorganization toward existing states, rather than permanent new identities created by matching. Neither nearest-neighbor specificity, autonomous/inherited identity, new carry-corrected development nor general geometric convergence is established.

The next focused confirmation should repeat the matched local-state and surrounding-budget intervention with **chemistry and mechanics co-evolving**. Preserve actual initial physical fields, own controls, explicit matching amounts and unchanged timestep/source gates. That would test whether the allocation-sensitive behavior, including the newly detected cell-22 response, survives evolving contacts and mechanical feedback. A later settled challenge would address a separate timing question, but its local chemistry and amounts must be reported again rather than assumed still matched. **No new test is launched by this review.**

The prospective protocol, accepted parent studies, simulation sources, results ledger and manuscript remain unchanged. The coordinator's historical pending-review flag is preserved; acceptance is recorded in this separate assessment.
