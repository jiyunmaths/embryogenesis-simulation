THE EXPERIMENT

Local rules.
Collective behavior.

A generic 3D model couples deformable cell shapes, Gierer–Meinhardt activator–inhibitor signaling on the contact graph, a two-fate regulatory switch, and apical–basal polarity.

Cells divide along a shape-selected axis through progressive cytokinesis. The embryo outline and daughter identities are not prescribed.

Time and parameters are dimensionless. Identity thresholds do not establish irreversible commitment, and shape changes do not establish a developmental axis. Cavity fluid transport is not implemented.

Run starts or resumes the solver. Pause preserves the current state. To change parameters after starting, pause, edit, then Reset. Reset applies the edited parameters and starts again from a zygote.

The timeline inspects recorded frames; it does not rewind the solver. This local session is held in memory. Downloaded frames contain visualization data and parameters, not a checkpoint for exact continuation.
