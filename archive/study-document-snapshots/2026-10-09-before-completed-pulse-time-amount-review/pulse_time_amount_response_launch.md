It asks whether spatial chemical allocation still changes a recipient's immediate response when initial local chemistry, surrounding species totals and the physical context are held common.

Both measured recipient pairs and both measured surrounding-total levels are tested. Uniform scaling within each surrounding species preserves that species' relative spatial allocation while changing its total amount. The target pair remains unchanged.
